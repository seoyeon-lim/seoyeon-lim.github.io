Research in macro-housing draws on two complementary toolkits: empirical identification using household- and loan-level microdata and local economic variation, and quantitative models of heterogeneous households, housing, and mortgage debt. These notes cover empirical methods, housing models, and computational techniques, along with readings connecting these topics.
