Research in macro-housing draws on two complementary toolkits. Housing and mortgage markets are rich in data, and household- and loan-level micro data are becoming increasingly available, making careful empirical identification essential. At the same time, answering macro-housing questions often requires building quantitative models with heterogeneous households, illiquid housing, and mortgage debt, which in turn calls for appropriate computational methods. These notes are organized around these two pillars.
